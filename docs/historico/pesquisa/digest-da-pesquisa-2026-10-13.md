# Research digest: scanning software for the HP DeskJet Ink Advantage 2874 on a MacBook

Merged 2026-10-13 from 7 research fronts (research 2026-10-12) and their skeptic reviews. Tags: [CONFIRMED] skeptic confirmed it, or researcher rated it high with a primary source; [UNVERIFIED] no direct evidence (printer offline, untested, source unreachable); [INFERENCE] reasoning only; [SKEPTIC-ADDED: x] found only by a skeptic, x = their confidence. Refuted claims are dropped and replaced by the skeptic's correction. "src:" keys resolve in section 11. ICC = ImageCaptureCore, EWS = printer web server, LN = macOS Local Network privacy, CLT = Command Line Tools, DTS = Apple developer support.

Limits: every front used the web, but the shared WebSearch budget ran out in all of them, so checks were direct fetches plus read-only inspection of this Mac; support.hp.com and HP Community were unreachable; the Mopria eSCL and Apple AirPrint specs were not read; no front probed the printer or the LAN. The UI researcher once ran HP.app --version by accident and briefly launched a throwaway SwiftUI app (removed). The scratchpad holds ~1.1 GB of research files (delete after use). MAC address and PPD UUID are device identifiers: keep them out of public repos.

## 0. Executive summary

1. Hardware: flatbed-only CIS (216x297 mm, 1200 dpi optical), no ADF, no scan button, 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Every page is a manual lid lift; speed is unmeasured (sibling ~20 s/page at 300 dpi B&W, colour maybe 2x slower): thousands of pages = tens to hundreds of hours; an ADF scanner suits bulk documents.
2. Identity: HP 6W7G2A / SKU 2874, queue "HP DeskJet 2800 series [000001]", MAC <MAC-DA-IMPRESSORA>, host HP<MAC-SEM-SEPARADORES>.local, firmware TIP1CN2446AR.
3. eSCL is very likely at family level (Mopria Scan certification, HPLIP scan-type 9, a 2024 dump advertising _uscan._tcp on 8080) but unobserved on this offline unit: Spike 0 first.
4. macOS 15.7.3 has one scanner driver, Apple's AirScan (eSCL); Image Capture, Preview, ICC, scanline and NAPS2's Apple driver all depend on it. "Scanning support: No" is inconclusive.
5. Direction: direct eSCL client in Python (httpx) behind an injected ScannerGateway; JPEG page masters; PDFs built on the Mac; ICC only as an optional out-of-process adapter; skip SANE.
6. Adopt now, build thin later: Image Capture, NAPS2 and the HP app already scan. Custom code is justified for the session model, crash-safe journal, PDF/JPG switch, naming, background post-processing, rescan UX; not for capture or multi-photo splitting alone.
7. LN privacy: only Apple's Terminal.app/SSH is exempt (not VS Code or Claude Code shells); ad-hoc rebuilds reset app grants. Test from Terminal.app, VPN off.
8. Storage: 2.3-3.2 GB free of 245 GB: external output volume + free-space guard.
9. PDF: img2pdf 0.6.3 + pikepdf 10.17.0 keep JPEG bytes (Pillow re-encodes); normalise JFIF+EXIF dpi; atomic page files.
10. OCR: background v1.1; Tesseract (por data missing, 2 MB) or Vision; never hand-roll the text layer.
11. Tooling: no Xcode.app; kebab-case Python modules cannot be imported: a naming exception is needed.
12. Moving target: macOS 27 shipped 2026-09-14; HP supports macOS <=13; NAPS2 8.5.x is days old: record builds.

## 3. Direct eSCL, SANE, WSD, ready-made clients

- Flow (sane-airscan, NAPS2, escl-scanner-cli agree): browse _uscan._tcp/_uscans._tcp; base scheme://host:port/<rs stripped, default eSCL>/; GET ScannerCapabilities; GET ScannerStatus (Idle); POST ScanJobs (text/xml) -> 201 + Location (use only its path); GET <Location>/NextDocument until the image (503 = not ready); a platen job yields one document, then 404; DELETE. sane-airscan skips the pre-POST status check. 2874 behaviour unobserved. [CONFIRMED] src: SAIR, NAPS2-RAW, ESCLCLI
- ScanSettings: pwg=http://www.pwg.org/schemas/2010/12/sm, scan=http://schemas.hp.com/imaging/escl/2011/05/03; pwg:Version 2.0; ScanRegions with literal escl:ThreeHundredthsOfInches (1/300 inch at any dpi; Width/Height from MaxWidth/MaxHeight, ~2551x3508 expected [INFERENCE]); InputSource Platen; ColorMode; DocumentFormat; X/YResolution; no Duplex for platen; compact one-line XML in canonical order (Version, Intent?, ScanRegions, InputSource, CompressionFactor?, ColorMode, DocumentFormat, DocumentFormatExt?, X/YResolution). escl-scanner-cli sends another order plus Duplex=false and works on a DeskJet 4640: strictness varies. [CONFIRMED] src: SAIR, GOMFP, ESCLCLI
- Status/timeouts: ScanJobs 201/400/409/503; poll NextDocument every 1-2 s; 404 ends the job only after >=1 page (404/410 mean "retry" on some Xerox); always DELETE (some Kyocera stay busy); one job at a time; Processing/Testing/Stopped = busy, Down = offline. Timeouts: sane-airscan 20 s caps/status, 30 s POST, none for NextDocument, 503 retried 10x (30x) at 1 s; NAPS2 5 s connect, 10 s calls, 120 s NextDocument (60 s stall), 503 every 2 s. [CONFIRMED] src: SAIR, NAPS2-RAW, AIRSANE
- HP quirks (mostly other models; keep a quirk table): DeskJet 2540 rejected multi-line XML; rs "eSCL" vs "/eSCL"; HP_Compact_Server needs "Host: localhost"; lowercase <!doctype (NAPS2 #654); OfficeJet Pro 9010 over-exposure (scan:Brightness 500); Smart Tank 7306 octet-stream + heavy JPEG compression. DeskJet 5000 "edge-detect crop" is one user's claim (closed not planned); still scan the full platen and crop locally. BlackAndWhite1 may give 409 (NAPS2 retries Grayscale8); send CompressionFactor only if CompressionFactorSupport is advertised. [CONFIRMED as reports] src: SAIR-GH, NAPS2-GH, NAPS2-RAW
- TLS: 2700 siblings expose HTTP 8080 + HTTPS 443 (airscan-discover only); self-signed; sane-airscan never verifies, NAPS2 accepts and falls back to HTTP; Python 3.12.3 (OpenSSL 3.0.13) requires TLS >=1.2, so old stacks may fail even with verify off. Default to HTTP on the LAN. [CONFIRMED] src: LAUNCHPAD, NAPS2-RAW, SAIR
- SANE: Homebrew sane-backends 1.4.0_2 has no escl (needs Avahi, Linux-only in Homebrew), so scanimage cannot reach an eSCL printer. Corrected: MacPorts has an escl variant needing avahi, and three immature community ports exist (Danut26, mikebean233, airscan-wsd-bridge, which also bridges WSD-only scanners to eSCL); skipping SANE still stands. [CONFIRMED] src: SANE-BREW, MACPORTS, COMMUNITY-PORTS
- Formats: request image/jpeg per page (sane-airscan decodes only JPEG/PNG/TIFF/BMP); build PDFs locally and losslessly (§6). [CONFIRMED] src: SAIR, NAPS2-RAW
- Clients (2026-10-12): sane-airscan 0.99.38 (GPL), NAPS2 8.5.1 (best-maintained eSCL implementation), scanlib 1.3.2, escl-scanner-cli 0.2.1 (stale), node-hp-scan-to 1.11.1 (no 2700/2800). No general eSCL package on PyPI: write a thin client (~300 lines). [SKEPTIC-ADDED: measured] References: MacScannerX (SwiftUI, Apache-2.0, builds with CLT, tested on a DeskJet 2300), hwisu/DCP-T420W (MIT, PITFALLS.md), openscan-hub, scanner-mcp, airscan-wsd-bridge, escl-async; borrow only MIT/Apache, read NAPS2/sane-airscan only. [CONFIRMED] src: SAIR-GH, NAPS2-GH, ESCLCLI, NODE-HP, MACSCANNERX, GH-TOPICS
- [SKEPTIC-ADDED] HP LEDM fallback (unverified for the 2800): /DevMgmt/DiscoveryTree.xml, /Scan/ScanCaps, POST /Scan/Jobs, poll BinaryURL; HPLIP rates 2600 as LEDM, 2700/2800 as eSCL. [UNVERIFIED] src: MACSCANNERX, HPLIPDB

[CONFIRMED] src: AIRSANE
- Discovery: python-zeroconf 0.151.5 (LGPL, not installed; TXT keys case-insensitive, HP sends "UUID"; V4Only), dns-sd, ippfind, getaddrinfo(host.local), NWBrowser in Swift. Persist UUID/host/port/scheme/rs; try the cached endpoint (5 s), race fresh mDNS, back off 1-30 s, manual host:port, DHCP reservation. ## 5. Flatbed bulk workflow, UX, capacity

- Model: NAPS2 Batch Scan, ExactScan "Ask for scanning more pages", Epson "Add page", ScanSnap continuous scan (sheet-fed analogy) and VueScan Auto repeat all use preset -> one trigger -> pages append to an open document -> explicit finish, with thumbnails to rotate/reorder/delete. Use Session > Document > Page with separate append/finish keys; photo preset = one scan, one file. [CONFIRMED] src: NAPS2-DOCS, EXACTSCAN, VENDOR-DOCS, HAMRICK
- Trigger: a flatbed has no auto-start (ExactScan's is ADF-only; ICC documentLoaded exists only on feeders), so use a key, pedal or timer. USB HID pedals type keystrokes [INFERENCE; no model chosen]. In-window keys need no permission; NSEvent monitors, CGEventTap, pynput need Accessibility/Input Monitoring; Carbon RegisterEventHotKey needs none (Option-only chords failed on 15.0-15.1, fixed in 15.2: prefer non-Option). Debounce, ignore key repeat, one queued trigger, warn on focus loss. NAPS2 shortcuts already let a pedal drive it. [CONFIRMED] src: EXACTSCAN, ICCHDR, KEY-DOCS, FORUMS
- Journal: each page is its own JPEG (temp file, flush, F_FULLFSYNC, atomic rename) + SQLite manifest; PDF at "finish"; a crash loses at most the in-flight page [design INFERENCE]. macOS fsync does not flush the drive cache and Python's os.fsync is plain fsync: call fcntl.fcntl(fd, F_FULLFSYNC) (51), fall back to fsync, fsync the parent dir. exFAT has no journal: use APFS/HFS+ Journaled or verify on resume. Names: date + zero-padded counter via exclusive create, digits for the max job (5 for 20,000), lowercase, <=32 characters. Keep two copies (LoC): archive loose pages, delete only after a verified second copy. [CONFIRMED facts] src: DURABILITY, FADGI, LOC
- Throughput (A4 colour 300 dpi): DeskJet 2540 20 s, 2710e ~24 s, Epson WF-645 over Wi-Fi 19.5 s (Epson colour/B&W: 19.5/9.1 s at 300 dpi, 71.5/27.4 s at 600; ~2012 hardware); 2540: 157 s for a 1200 dpi photo. Researcher estimate for the 2874: 300 dpi 19-25 s, 600 dpi 65-90 s, 100-145 pages/h with lid handling (1,000 pages 7-10 h; 20,000 pages 140-200 h). Skeptic: UNVERIFIED; if the HP figures were mono, colour could take 40-50 s (60-75 pages/h; 14-17 h per 1,000). Plan 20-50 s/page until measured. B&W at 300 dpi halves time and shrinks text pages ~16x but needs bilevel (default Grayscale8 + host threshold). [INFERENCE for the 2874] src: THROUGHPUT, HAMRICK, EXPERTREVIEWS
- Sizes (other devices/synthetic; calibrate): A4 colour 0.65-1.4 MB at 300 dpi, 1.7-5.2 MB at 600; 1,000 / 5,000 / 20,000 pages at 300 dpi = 0.65-1.4 / 3.3-7 / 13-28 GB; uncompressed TIFF 26.1 MB/page. [INFERENCE] src: SIZES, HAMRICK
- Disk: 245 GB internal, ~1.4-3.2 GB free (2.3-2.4 GB on 2026-10-13), swap 7,087 of 8,192 MB used, no external volume: output-folder picker defaulting to external, free-space floor that pauses scans, pages-left forecast. ## 6. Image and PDF pipeline

- Pipeline (design INFERENCE): scanner JPEG = page master; lossless normalise (JFIF units=1 + density AND EXIF resolution, sRGB ICC if absent, EXIF DateTimeDigitized); atomic page write (§5); at finish img2pdf.convert(paths, layout_fun=get_fixed_dpi_layout_fun((dpi, dpi)), rotation=Rotation.ifvalid, engine=Engine.pikepdf, outputstream=*.pdf.part), verify with pikepdf, os.replace; JPG mode finalises the same JPEG; re-encode only when geometry/clean-up is on (decode once, edit, encode once ~q92). [INFERENCE] src: IMG2PDF, OCRMYPDF
- img2pdf 0.6.3 (LGPL-3.0; no release for 11 months) + pikepdf 10.17.0 (MPL-2.0, 2 days old; cp312 macosx_15 arm64 wheel; Python >=3.11): DCT passthrough byte-identical (OCRmyPDF relies on and tests it; not run here); EXIF Orientation -> /Rotate; size = px/dpi x 72 (96 dpi if none). ## 8. Cross-front conflicts and resolutions

1. HP app Bonjour types: one front read Info.plist as lacking _uscan (so HP's client finds printers via IPP/HTTP); three fronts (two by re-inspection) say _uscan._tcp/_uscans._tcp are declared. Resolution: treat as declared, drop the IPP inference, verify with `plutil -p /Applications/HP.app/Contents/Info.plist` (S0-12).
2. Why offline: HP says Auto-Off is off while wireless is on (so manual power-off, Wi-Fi or network change) vs a TIP that it can be enabled plus a 0.3 W auto-off state. Undecidable: check the power light.
3. Sleep adjustable? HP generic sheet "cannot be changed" vs 2700 guide EWS Energy Save Mode vs community snippet 5/10/15 min (low grade): read the 2874's EWS.
4. eSCL wording differed by front ("probable", "unconfirmed"): unified as family-level very likely, unit-level UNVERIFIED. "Scanning support: No" is inconclusive (unbound queue vs PPD made without APScanner keys vs MacScannerX "No" on an eSCL DeskJet 2300): gate on Image Capture.
5. Seconds per page: ~20 s (B&W sibling) vs colour 2.1x slower on the Epson: plan 20-50 s, measure. B&W: a bilevel preset halves time vs 1-bit failures over Wi-Fi: Grayscale8 + host threshold, test BlackAndWhite1.
6. Tools: SANE "dead end" vs MacPorts variant and community ports (skipping SANE stands); NAPS2 profiles toolbar on Mac (claimed twice) vs source (Windows only); "no free multi-photo split" and "no one-key PDF loop" vs Image Capture/HP Multi-Item/VueScan and `-n N --waitscan` (gaps narrowed to journal, early finish, PDF/JPG toggle); the NAPS2 console has no auto-crop flag and always exits 0.
7. Terminal exemption: earlier fronts called VS Code/iTerm an inference; Apple DTS says only Terminal/SSH; Cursor/Claude Code reports; one reader disputes: test in Terminal.app (S0-11) or use the system-wide default.
8. Baselines: "pin 15.7.x" is obsolete; Python 3.12.3 coexists with 3.13.7/3.11.6; free disk 1.4-3.2 GB (10-12) vs 2.3-2.7 GB (10-13) as research writes to the scratchpad: plan for <=3 GB.
9. ICC vs direct eSCL: only direct eSCL has JPEG-quality and scanner-PDF controls (§10). Deleting loose JPEGs after assembly vs LoC two copies: archive first. JPEG size ratios were not reproduced: placeholders only.
10. Kebab-case rule vs Python imports: needs a user decision. "HP software cannot be a dependency" vs a current HP app: a design choice. Dropped refuted claim: HP India "2876 dual-band" (that page is the 2986).
